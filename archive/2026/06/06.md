### [eval] [One Developer Ran the Numbers on Claude and rsync Bug Rates](https://wwwatch.dev/journal/2026-06-06/did-claude-make-rsync-buggier-one-dev-ran-the-numbers)

### [coding_agent] [The Handoff Doc Trick That Turns AI Notes Into Real Docs](https://wwwatch.dev/journal/2026-06-06/commit-claude-handoff-docs-as-real-documentation)

### [infra_api] [Vercel Sandbox Gets Persistent Storage That Outlives Any Single Run](https://wwwatch.dev/journal/2026-06-06/vercel-sandbox-drives-persistent-storage-private-beta)

Vercel Sandbox now supports Drives in private beta, giving sandboxes persistent, attachable storage with a lifecycle independent from the sandbox itself. Create a drive once, mount it at start, and it survives after the sandbox stops.

### [framework] [LlamaFactory v0.9.5 Adds Qwen3, Gemma4, and Transformers v5 Support](https://wwwatch.dev/journal/2026-06-06/llamafactory-v095-qwen3-gemma4-transformers-v5)

LlamaFactory v0.9.5 ships primary support for Qwen3.5, Qwen3.6, and Gemma4 models alongside compatibility with Transformers v5. The release also adds new model support, an EAFT loss option, and FP8 fixes.
