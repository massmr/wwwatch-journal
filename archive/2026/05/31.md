## May 31, 2026

Two releases worth flagging today. Hugging Face Transformers v5.9.0 adds Cohere's MoE model, a hierarchical reasoning language model, and Parakeet TDT, but breaks SAM3 and EdgeTAM text embedding input contracts, so check that before upgrading. LlamaFactory v0.9.5 extends the compatibility story further, landing Qwen3.5, Qwen3.6, and Gemma 4 support alongside a Transformer Engine backend for FP8 training. Dify v1.14.2 also ships a quiet but important security fix for tenant isolation, worth reviewing before your next deploy.
